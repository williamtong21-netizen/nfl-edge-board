More angles: trap watch & alternate lines
    
Trap watch

    
Lines that look too good for the side casual bettors love, while the evidence (line moving the other way, key numbers, cheaper prices, the models, and the sharpest book when we have its odds) points the other way. Moves that injury news explains are discounted. It's a lean, not a lock, so the record of fading these is tracked below.
